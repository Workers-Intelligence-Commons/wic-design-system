supporting wpt

Nothing pays for owning the model

WPT (Workers’ Pre-trained Transformer) is a copyleft language model trained only on content whose license is clear. Training it costs compute, and compute costs money. This page explains how you can help, and what happens to what you give.

honest status

Draft. Items marked “to fill in” are placeholders until the platforms and the legal form are chosen. Until then, WPT is run by one person, and this page says so plainly.

in this orderWhat your support pays for

  1. 1GPU compute for training and evaluation runs.
  2. 2Storage and hosting for the corpus and the provenance registry.
  3. 3Infrastructure for the public test service, served without margin.

Nothing pays for owning the model. WPT stays copyleft, under AGPL-3.0-or-later, whatever happens to funding.

three waysWays to give

Money

  • Recurring or one-time donations: <Liberapay / Open Collective / association page link>
  • A donation is a gift. It buys no features, no priority, and no say over which sources enter the corpus. The rule stays: when a license is doubtful, the content is refused.

Compute

If you run a GPU program, a cluster or a cloud account and can grant credits or hours, write to <contact address>. Please say what hardware is offered and for how long. Compute grants are reported publicly the same way money is.

Work

Pull requests are welcome, with DCO sign-off required in CI. Contributions that are merged can earn service credits, as described in the service documentation. That is a separate mechanism from donations.

kept apart on purposeDonations and the paid service are different things

Donations

no counterpart

Unconditional. A gift to the commons, nothing in return.

Paid service

small margin

Usage credits, billed at a small margin, optional. Self-hosting is always possible, and anyone may offer the model as a paid service too.

Keeping the two apart keeps the project honest about what each euro is for.
