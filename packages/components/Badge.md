# Badge

A scrap of paper pasted barely crooked, its word in the gothic `cue`, saying a source's status in one word with its glyph, so colour is never alone. The consumer provides `status` and may override the word with `children`: `admitted` (■ on ink), `refused` (✕, red-ink on red-soft), `pending` (○ on ochre). `license` with `children` sets a license name in the typewriter face, underlined ("AGPL-3.0-or-later"). Do not invent other statuses: a source is admitted, refused or still pending a signed decision.
