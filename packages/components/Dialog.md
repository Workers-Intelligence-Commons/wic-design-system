# Dialog

A sheet of `paper-raised` pasted over the page, under the ink strip of a Sheet, with the page behind laid under a screen of paper. It is the browser's modal dialog: focus stays inside, the page behind is inert, and Escape closes it. The consumer provides `open`, `onClose`, `title` (set in the gothic `title`, and the dialog's accessible name) and `children`, and optionally `actions` (beside the title) and `closeLabel` (defaults to "Close"). The consumer owns `open`: Escape, the close button and a click on the screen around the sheet call `onClose` and change nothing themselves. `placement="center"` (default) sets the sheet over the middle of the page, at most 720px wide, and scrolls its body when it is taller than the page. `placement="side"` sets a sheet the height of the page against its left edge, for navigation such as a ConversationList.
