# Mark

The WPT, WIC or hub monogram, drawn from the logo files in the inks of the current theme, so it reverses by itself on dark grounds. The consumer provides `name` (`wpt`, `wic` or `hub`) and optionally `height` in pixels (default 32, never under 24), `tone` (`two-ink`, the default, or `red` for the single-ink mark) and `label`. Without a label the mark is hidden from screen readers, for when the name is written beside it; give a label when the mark stands alone. Keep clear space of half its height around it.
