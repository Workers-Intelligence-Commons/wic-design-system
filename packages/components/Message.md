# Message and MessageList

A conversation between a person and a model. `Message` sets the person's words on a raised scrap pasted to the right and the model's reply printed on the page behind a rule of ink, each under its lowercase italic label. The consumer provides `from` (`user` or `assistant`), `children` (the paragraphs), and optionally `label` (defaults to "you" or "assistant"), `streaming` (a red cursor at the end, and the message marked busy), `end` (`interrupted` or `failed`, said with its glyph and word) and `actions` (quiet buttons and a BranchSwitcher under the text), `details` (a short line about the message, such as its model and timings, typed at the end of the actions and shown while the message has the pointer or the focus; a tap focuses the message on a touch screen, and screen readers always read it) and `entering` (the message arrives pasted; set it on a message written while the reader watches, not on the messages already there when the list opens). `MessageList` holds the messages as a log that follows its end while a reply streams, unless the reader has scrolled up. It opens at its end and glides there afterwards, or jumps when the reader asks for reduced motion. Give it the height it scrolls in.
