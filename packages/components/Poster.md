# Poster

The main surface of a view, pasted on the page like a poster on a wall: raised paper with a light grain, cut edges and a strip of ochre tape at each top corner, pasted in when it appears. The consumer provides `children` and optionally `className`, which sets its size and place. The Poster sets no padding and no scrolling; its content scrolls inside it when the consumer gives it a height. Use one Poster per view, for what the view is about, such as a conversation. The tape is left out on narrow screens. A choice among a few parallel options is a Sheet, not a Poster.
