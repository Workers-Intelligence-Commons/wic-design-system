# Question

A sheet handed to the person when the conversation needs their answer before it goes on: an approval, a choice, a short form. Its label is a strip of ochre tape ("a question for you" unless `from` names who asks, such as a tool server), and the question is set in the serif `lead`. The consumer provides `question`, and optionally `children` (form controls or a list of choices), `actions` (the buttons) and `onSubmit`, which makes the sheet a form. Ask one thing per sheet, and put the button that answers yes or goes on first.
