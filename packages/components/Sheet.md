# Sheet

A cut page of `paper-raised` with a hand-cut strip across its top, holding one option among a few parallel ones (the ways to give, the steps of a process). The consumer provides `title` (one or two words, set in the gothic `title`), `children` (paragraphs, a short list, actions) and optionally `tone` for the strip: `ink` (default), `red` or `ochre`. Give parallel sheets different tones so they read as a set, and stack them in one column. A record of a source is a SourceCard, not a Sheet.
