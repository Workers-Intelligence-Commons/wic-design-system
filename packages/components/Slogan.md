# Slogan

A phrase played like a riff: every third word set larger and every fourth in the other ink, all on one baseline and never rotated, with cut bars of ink and ochre swinging around it. The slogan is the page's main heading (`h1`), and the plain sentence is kept as its accessible label. The consumer provides `children` as a plain string (six words or fewer), optionally a `kicker` (lowercase italics: who speaks, or which rule) and `tone`: `red` (default) or `ink`. One per page, with `space-12` of silence around it. Slogans state a rule the project actually holds, never a marketing claim.
