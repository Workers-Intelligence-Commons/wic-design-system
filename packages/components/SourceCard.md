# SourceCard

One sheet of the provenance ledger: the source's name in the gothic `title`, its status badge, and its facts with italic labels and typewritten values, under a hand-cut ink strip. The consumer provides `name` and `status`, and whatever of `license`, `credit`, `revision`, `checksum` and `decidedBy` is known; empty rows are left out. Write the license as its SPDX identifier or its own words, shorten checksums with an ellipsis, and state a refusal's reason in `decidedBy`. Stack cards in one column.
