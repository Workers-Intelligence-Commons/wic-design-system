# Status

The state of a conversation or of what serves it, in one word with its glyph on a crooked scrap, so colour is never alone: `running` (○ on ochre, turning, announced to screen readers), `waiting` (○ on ochre), `offline` (✕, red-ink on red-soft) and `done` (■ on ink). The consumer provides `kind` and may replace the word with `children` ("server offline"). The status of a source is a Badge, not a Status.
