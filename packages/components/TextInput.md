# TextInput, TextArea, Select and Checkbox

The form controls: a label in the gothic `cue` over a box ruled in `line-strong`, the one shape that takes `radius-1`, with the value in the plain reading face. The consumer provides `label` and the native props of the control, and optionally `hint` (one italic sentence under the box) and `error` (what is wrong, in red-ink after its ✕, announced as soon as it appears). The label, hint and error are tied to the control for screen readers; an `id` given by the consumer is kept. `TextArea` defaults to three rows, `Select` takes native `<option>` children, and `Checkbox` sets its label beside the box. Write labels as nouns in sentence case ("API key"), placeholders as an example value, and errors as a plain sentence that says what to change.
