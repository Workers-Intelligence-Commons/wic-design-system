# TextInput, TextArea, Select, Checkbox and Switch

The form controls: a label in the gothic `cue` over a box ruled in `line-strong`, the one shape that takes `radius-1`, with the value in the plain reading face. The consumer provides `label` and the native props of the control, and optionally `hint` (one italic sentence under the box) and `error` (what is wrong, in red-ink after its ✕, announced as soon as it appears). The label, hint and error are tied to the control for screen readers; an `id` given by the consumer is kept. `TextArea` defaults to three rows, `Select` takes native `<option>` children, and `Checkbox` sets its label beside the box. `Switch` is a checkbox announced as a switch, for a setting that takes effect at once without a form to submit: a square track whose knob slides right onto ochre when it is on, its label beside it in the gothic `cue`. Its hint says what turning it on does, and is set upright since it may take several sentences. Write labels as nouns in sentence case ("API key"), placeholders as an example value, and errors as a plain sentence that says what to change.
